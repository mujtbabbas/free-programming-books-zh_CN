# Reader's Guide — free-programming-books-zh_CN

## What this project is

This is an **index of free programming books in Chinese**
（免费的编程中文书籍索引）: a single, browsable catalogue linking to freely
available Chinese-language books, translations and tutorials across the whole
spectrum of programming, compiled by the community (contributions welcome).
It is the Chinese companion to the well-known free-programming-books family.

## How the content is organised

- [`README.md`](README.md) — the index itself (in Chinese), with a
  **目录 (table of contents)** and two kinds of sections:
  - **By topic:** 操作系统 (operating systems), AI, 智能系统 (intelligent
    systems), 分布式系统 (distributed systems), 编译原理 (compilers),
    函数式概念 (functional programming concepts), 计算机图形学 (computer
    graphics), WEB服务器 (web servers), 版本控制 (version control),
    编辑器 (editors), databases (NoSQL, PostgreSQL, MySQL), 管理和监控
    (administration & monitoring), 项目相关 (project practice), 设计模式
    (design patterns), Web, 大数据 (big data), 编程艺术 (the art of
    programming), 游戏引擎 (game engines), 算法 (algorithms), and more,
    including 置顶 (pinned highlights) at the top.
  - **By language/platform:** sections such as Android, APP, AWK and C/C++,
    each listing that language's free Chinese books.
- `what-non-programming-books-should-programmers-read.md` — the companion
  list: non-programming books recommended for programmers (drawn from the
  classic Stack Overflow question on the subject).
- `CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md` and `CONTRIBUTING.md` — community and submission
  rules; `LICENSE` — GPLv3.

## How to use it

1. Open the README's 目录 and jump to your topic or language.
2. Each entry links out to the book itself (hosted by its author/publisher) —
   this repo is the index, not the bookshelf.
3. Know a free Chinese programming book that is missing? Contributions are
   invited; see the README's 参与交流 (community) section.
